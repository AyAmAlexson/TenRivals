Accessibility

We want everyone — including customers who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive tools — to be able to use Tennis Rivals Shop successfully.

Our commitment

We aim to align the storefront with widely recognised guidelines such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA where reasonably achievable. New templates and major updates are reviewed with accessibility in mind.

Ongoing work

E-commerce sites evolve (new products, third-party widgets, design changes). Some areas may not yet meet every success criterion. We welcome specific feedback so we can prioritise fixes.

Need help?

If you cannot access part of the site or complete a task (e.g. search, checkout, account), please email andy.rivals@tenrivals.com with the page URL and what you were trying to do. We will work with you to provide the information or service by another means where possible.

Third-party content

Some features (e.g. embedded maps, online card checkout widgets from our payment partner, payment terminals in person, or external links) are provided by third parties. Their accessibility is governed by those providers.
