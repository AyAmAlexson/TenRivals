Cookie policy

How Tennis Rivals Shop uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. This policy should be read together with our Privacy policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a site. We also use similar technologies (e.g. local storage) where they help the shop function. Georgian law and good practice require transparency and, for non-essential cookies, appropriate consent where applicable.

Types we use

  • Strictly necessary: Required for the site to work — e.g. session, security (CSRF), cart or checkout state, cookie consent choice. These are usually exempt from consent under typical interpretations because they are essential to provide the service you request.
  • Functional: Remember preferences (e.g. language, display options). We only use these if you enable them or if they are low-impact and aligned with our privacy notice.
  • Analytics & performance: Help us understand traffic and errors (e.g. page views, anonymised usage). Non-essential analytics are used only after opt-in through any cookie notice or preference tools we publish, as required by applicable rules.
  • Payment & checkout: Our payment provider may set its own cookies or storage during card payment on the website. Those are governed by the provider’s policy and are needed to complete a transaction you initiate.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break login, cart, or checkout. When we provide a cookie preference control on the site, use it to manage non-essential categories.

Updates

We may update this policy when we change tools or the law requires. Check the date below.
