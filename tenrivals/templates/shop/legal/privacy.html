Privacy policy

How Tennis Rivals Shop collects and uses personal data when you use our website, account, or checkout. We target customers in Georgia and process data under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection and related rules. Online card payment on the site is not live yet; payment is usually taken on delivery or by bank transfer as described on Payment.

This page is for transparency. It does not replace individual legal advice. If you need a formal data subject request, use the contact below.

1. Data controller

The controller is IE Andrey Molodenko (individual entrepreneur; identification code 100382729), operator of Tennis Rivals Shop, address: Georgia, Tbilisi, E. Bejanishvili Street 23, apt. 19. Contact: andy.rivals@tenrivals.com and the channels on our Contacts page.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

  • Account & identity: name, email, phone (if provided), login identifiers.
  • Orders & checkout: delivery address, cart contents, order references, payment method choice, and messages you add at checkout. We do not collect card numbers through the website checkout today (online card capture is not enabled). If you pay by card on delivery, the courier’s terminal or bank handles card data under their rules; we may receive transaction references and amounts needed for receipts and accounting. For bank transfers, we process account and reference data needed to match your payment.
  • Communications: messages you send us (email, social channels we monitor for support).
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser type, pages viewed, timestamps — from server logs and, if enabled, analytics cookies (see our Cookie policy).

3. Purposes & legal grounds

We use data to: perform our contract with you (arrange payment and delivery, support orders); operate accounts; meet tax, accounting, and other legal obligations under Georgian law; secure the site and prevent fraud; and, if you opt in, send marketing. Where the law requires consent (for example certain cookies or direct marketing), we will ask for it separately.

4. Sharing

We may share data with: banks and payment/terminal providers (to complete card-at-delivery or transfer payments); delivery partners; hosting and IT providers; and advisers (e.g. accountants) where required. They may process data only on our instructions or as required by law. We do not sell personal data.

5. Transfers outside Georgia

Our primary operations and customers are in Georgia. Some tools (e.g. email, hosting, or future online payment gateways) may involve processing in other countries. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards required by Georgian law and contractual clauses with providers.

6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above — for example order and fiscal records for periods set by Georgian tax and accounting rules, account data while your account is active, and logs for security for a limited time. When retention ends, we delete or anonymise data where feasible.

7. Your rights (Georgia)

Under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, you generally have rights to request information about processing, access, rectification, updating, blocking, or deletion of your personal data where the law allows, and to object to processing in prescribed cases. To exercise these rights, email andy.rivals@tenrivals.com. You may also contact the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia (personaldata.ge) with questions or complaints about lawful processing.

8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie policy.

9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (HTTPS, access controls, agreements with processors). When we enable card payment on the website, entry will be handled by certified payment partners; until then, card data for your purchase is processed on the courier’s terminal or by your bank. No online system is perfectly secure — use a strong password and protect your devices.

10. Minors

The shop is aimed at adults who can enter into contracts under Georgian law. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental involvement; if you believe we have, contact us and we will address it.

11. Updates

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page; material changes will be reflected in the date below.
