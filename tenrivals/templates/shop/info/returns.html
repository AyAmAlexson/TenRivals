The Fair Play Policy

In tennis, “Fair Play” is everything. We apply that same logic to our shop. If a piece of gear doesn’t feel right or doesn’t meet your expectations, we’re here to find a solution.

14-day return window

Under Georgian law and our own quality standards, you have 14 days from the date of purchase to return or exchange your items.

Conditions for a successful return

To maintain the highest quality for all our customers, we ask that:

  • Original packaging: Items must be in their original, undamaged packaging with all tags and labels attached.
  • Condition: Items must be unused and in “as new” condition.
  • Rackets: The plastic wrap on the handle must be intact.
  • Apparel / shoes: Must be unworn (except for trying them on) and unwashed.
  • The strung racket rule: Once a racket has been custom-strung for you, it cannot be returned unless there is a manufacturing defect.

How to initiate a return

Send us a DM on Instagram or an email with your order number. We will review the request and, if it meets the criteria, we will arrange a pickup or exchange. Your growth as a player is our priority, and we’ll work hard to ensure you end up with the gear that actually fits your game.
