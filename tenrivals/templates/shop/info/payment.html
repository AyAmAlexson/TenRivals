Payment & delivery in Tbilisi and across Georgia

Tenrivals is a tennis equipment shop in Tbilisi, Georgia. We sell in-stock items with free delivery in Tbilisi, arrange couriers across the country, and run preorders from the EU and USA with official customs clearance. Every sale is backed by 100% official documentation — no grey imports.

Official documentation

Every purchase you make comes with an official fiscal receipt (RS.ge). This is your ultimate guarantee that the equipment is 100% original and that you are dealing with a registered, responsible business.

Online card payment at checkout is not available yet (in development). You cannot pay by card on the website today. When we launch it, this page and checkout will be updated and we will process card data only through a licensed payment partner, as described in our Privacy policy.

Accepted payment methods (today)

For now you can settle your order as follows:

  • Card on delivery (POS terminal): Our courier can bring a mobile POS terminal. Pay with a major credit or debit card when you receive your order.
  • Cash on delivery: Pay in GEL directly to the courier when you receive your order.
  • Bank transfer: Where we offer it (e.g. club purchases or pre-orders), we provide an official invoice with bank details for a direct transfer before or after delivery, as agreed.

All prices on the website are listed in GEL (Georgian Lari) and include all applicable taxes. For legal terms of purchase (when the contract is formed, refunds, etc.), see Terms & conditions.

Delivery in Tbilisi & Georgia

In-stock orders are packed in Tbilisi. City customers get free delivery inside Tbilisi. For Batumi, Kutaisi, and other regions we use trusted couriers — ask us for timing and cost before you confirm.

Preorder from the EU or USA is quoted separately: we confirm landed cost, timeline, and customs paperwork before you commit. That keeps imports transparent for players and clubs across Georgia.
