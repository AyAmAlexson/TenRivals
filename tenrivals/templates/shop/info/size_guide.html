Precision Fitting for Every Player

Tennis is a game of precision, and that starts with your gear. A grip that is too small or shoes that are too tight can lead to tennis elbow or blisters that keep you off the court.

Racket grip sizes

Most adult players use a Grip 2 (4 1/4″) or Grip 3 (4 3/8″).

The rule of thumb: When holding the racket in a continental grip, you should be able to fit the index finger of your other hand in the gap between your fingertips and the palm of your hand.
