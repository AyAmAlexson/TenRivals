How to choose tennis racket weight in Tbilisi

A short, practical guide for club players in Tbilisi and Georgia: how racket mass relates to power, control, and arm comfort, and how Tenrivals can help you try models we keep in stock or preorder from the EU.

Why weight matters

Heavier frames (roughly 300 g and up strung) usually absorb more vibration and plough through the ball on defense. Lighter frames (under 285 g) accelerate faster for whippy topspin and quick reactions at net. Spec sheets list unstrung weight — add about 15–20 g for strings and dampener.

Match weight to your game

  • Beginners / juniors moving to adult frames: start toward the light end for easier timing.
  • All-court intermediates: mid-weight rackets (≈285–305 g strung) balance stability and manoeuvrability.
  • Advanced baseliners: often prefer heavier heads or higher swingweight even if static weight is moderate.
