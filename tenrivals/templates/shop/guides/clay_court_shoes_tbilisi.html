Clay-court tennis shoes for Tbilisi clubs

Georgian outdoor courts are often har-tru or slow hard — you still want a herringbone-style outsole for grip and slides. Here is what to look for when you buy tennis shoes in Tbilisi or preorder from the EU through Tenrivals.

Outsole pattern

True clay patterns use full herringbone so clay releases from the grooves. Many “all-court” shoes work on local courts, but dedicated clay soles feel safer on loose top dressing.

Upper & durability

Sliding wears the medial forefoot — look for reinforced toe bumpers (HEAD, Wilson, Babolat, ASICS, Nike, adidas all offer clay-specific lines). If you train daily, rotate two pairs to extend life.
